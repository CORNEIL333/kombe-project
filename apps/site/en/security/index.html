Trust is built.

KÓMBE does not hold your money: it keeps the group’s truth. Here is how that truth is protected.

01Our safeguards

Eight principles, enforced by the server.

Sign-in without a password.

A 6-digit code, single-use and valid for a few minutes, is sent to your address (or your number). Nothing to remember, nothing to steal over your shoulder. Sessions expire on their own.

Checked by several people.

A contribution is declared by one person and confirmed by another. Nobody confirms their own declaration: the server refuses it, whatever screen is used.

A register that is never erased.

Every event is added to the group’s log and chained by a SHA-256 fingerprint: changing or deleting a line breaks the chain, which shows. A mistake is corrected by a new entry.

Each group within its own boundary.

A group’s data can only be read by its members, according to their roles. The separation is enforced down to the database, not just on screen.

Masked numbers.

Full numbers are only visible to the group’s committee. Other members see a masked number.

Offline, in full clarity.

Without a network, what you enter stays on the phone and is marked “not sent yet”. Nothing is shown as confirmed before the server answers.

Protected in transit.

Exchanges go over HTTPS, with strict security headers (HSTS, content policy) that block foreign scripts.

Verifiable minutes.

The minutes of a meeting are generated from the recorded events and carry a fingerprint: they are approved at the next meeting.

02What we do not do

What KÓMBE will never do.

  • Hold, move or block the group’s money.
  • Access your Mobile Money or bank accounts.
  • Sell your data or show advertising.
  • Set tracking cookies.
  • Give financial advice.

03Report a vulnerability

Found a weakness?

Please report it to us before any public disclosure, with the steps to reproduce it. Do not access other people’s data beyond what is strictly necessary to demonstrate the problem.
