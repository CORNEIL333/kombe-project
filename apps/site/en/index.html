09Questions

What we are often asked.

Does KÓMBE hold the group’s money?

No. Money moves between members as before (cash, Mobile Money, bank transfer). KÓMBE keeps the register: who declared, who confirmed, whose turn it is.

Who can confirm a contribution?

An authorised member of the group, never the person who declared it. The group’s rules set who confirms and who checks.

What if the network drops during the meeting?

Declarations and the roll call stay on the phone, clearly marked “not sent yet”, then go out when the network returns.

Can a confirmed contribution be changed?

It is never erased: a correction is added to the history and the original stays visible.

How do I join a group?

The committee invites you with a code or a link. Create your account with your email address, then send your request: the committee approves it.
