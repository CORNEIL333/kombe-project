01Getting started

Six guides to begin.

1

Create your account

No password to remember.

  1. Open kombe.vercel.app or the Android app.
  2. Enter your email address (or your number, if SMS is open).
  3. Type in the 6-digit code you received: your account is created.
  4. Give the name the members of your groups know you by.
2

Create a tontine

For the president or the founder of the group.

  1. From “My tontines”, tap “Create a tontine”.
  2. Follow the steps: name, frequency, contribution, order of turns, committee, rules, review.
  3. Then share the invitation link (on WhatsApp, for example).
3

Join a group

With the code or the link received from the committee.

  1. Tap “Join with a code”, or open the link you received.
  2. Check the group name shown, then send your request.
  3. The committee approves your membership: the group then appears in “My tontines”.
4

Declare a contribution

Cash, Orange Money, MTN MoMo, bank…

  1. Open the group, then your dues.
  2. Tap “Declare”, choose the payment method and add the reference if you have one.
  3. Your declaration awaits the treasurer’s confirmation: you can see its status at any time.
5

Confirm as treasurer

Confirmation is done by another person.

  1. Open the group’s “Contributions” tab (or the committee space on a computer).
  2. Check each declaration, then confirm it or reject it with a reason.
  3. You can never confirm your own declaration.
6

Working without a network

During a meeting with no connection.

  1. Take the roll call and enter declarations as usual.
  2. They stay on the phone, marked “not sent yet”.
  3. They go out automatically when the network returns; nothing is shown as confirmed before that.

02Frequently asked questions

Answers to common questions.

Does KÓMBE hold the group’s money?

No. Money moves between members as before (cash, Mobile Money, bank transfer). KÓMBE keeps the register: who declared, who confirmed, whose turn it is.

I did not receive my code.

Check the address you entered and your spam folder, then ask for a new code. The code is only valid for a few minutes.

My membership is “pending”.

That is normal: a committee member must approve each new membership. Let them know if the wait drags on.

Can a contribution already confirmed be corrected?

Yes, through a correction added to the history. The original entry stays visible: nothing is erased silently.

Can I use KÓMBE on an iPhone or a computer?

Yes, with the web app in the browser. The committee also has the committee space, designed for a big screen.

Who sees my phone number?

Your group’s committee. Other members see your number masked.
