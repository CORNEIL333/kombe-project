Making every tontine clearer.

The tontine is one of the finest inventions of collective trust. KÓMBE gives it a register worthy of it.

01Why KÓMBE

A notebook, messages, doubts.

In many groups, the accounts live in a notebook, a spreadsheet and dozens of messages. When a doubt arises — who paid, when, how much — it weighs on the treasurer and on everyone’s trust.

KÓMBE replaces this fog with a single register: each contribution is declared, confirmed by another person, and stays visible. The money itself moves as before.

02Our principles

What guides every decision.

The group decides.

The rules, the order of turns and the committee belong to the group. KÓMBE applies them; it does not invent them.

No money with us.

KÓMBE is neither a bank nor a payment institution. It keeps the register and never touches the funds.

Nothing is erased silently.

A mistake is corrected by a new entry; the original stays visible to all.

Designed for real life.

Patchy network, modest phones, in-person meetings: the app works offline and stays light.

Real figures.

No made-up testimonials, figures or users: everything you see in KÓMBE comes from your group.

In your languages.

French, English and Pidgin in the web app; French and English in the Android app; more languages will follow.

03Where we are

A pilot phase.

KÓMBE is currently a pilot version: the web app, the Android app and the committee space are open, and they improve with the first groups. Direct support is in preparation.
