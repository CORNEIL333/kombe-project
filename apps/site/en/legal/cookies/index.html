Cookie policy.

Short, because there is little to say: KÓMBE sets no tracking cookies. This English translation is provided for information; the French version prevails.

In short

  • No cookies are set by KÓMBE: no advertising, no audience measurement, no social network.
  • No third-party tracking tool is loaded: the site’s security policy only allows our own scripts.
  • That is why no consent banner is shown to you.

What we keep on your device

To work, the web app keeps a little information in your browser’s local storage. It does not leave your device, except what you enter offline, which is sent to the server when the network returns.

NameUseDuration
kombe.sessionKeep you signed in to the web appUntil expiry or sign-out
kombe.langueRemember the chosen languageUntil deleted
kombe.fileAttenteEntries made offline, waiting to be sentUntil they are sent
kombe.dashboard.sessionSign-in to the committee space (current tab)Tab closed
kombe.bureau.groupeGroup chosen in the committee spaceUntil deleted
kombe.pushWeb.appareilDevice registered for notifications on this browser, if you turn them onUntil you turn them off

If you turn on notifications on this browser, it installs a small KÓMBE program that displays them and opens the app when tapped; it keeps no data. “Turn off” in your preferences removes it.

The Android app likewise keeps your session in the phone’s protected keychain and your offline drafts in a local database.

How to delete it

Sign out from the app, or clear the site data in your browser settings. On Android, “Clear data” for the KÓMBE app deletes everything it keeps on the phone. Warning: offline entries not yet sent would then be lost.

If this changes

If we ever had to use a non-essential cookie, it would only be set after your explicit consent, and this page would be updated.
