166|What we are often
167|asked.
168|Does KÓMBE hold the group’s money?
169|No. Money moves between members as before (cash, Mobile Money, bank transfer). KÓMBE keeps the register: who declared, who confirmed, whose turn it is.
170|Who can confirm a contribution?
171|An authorised member of the group, never the person who declared it. The group’s rules set who confirms and who checks.
172|What if the network drops during the meeting?
173|Declarations and the roll call stay on the phone, clearly marked “not sent yet”, then go out when the network returns.
174|Can a confirmed contribution be changed?
175|It is never erased: a correction is added to the history and the original stays visible.
176|How do I join a group?
177|The committee invites you with a code or a link. Create your account with your email address, then send your request: the committee approves it.
