19|In short
20|No cookies
21|are set by KÓMBE: no advertising, no audience measurement, no social network.
22|No third-party tracking tool is loaded: the site’s security policy only allows our own scripts.
23|That is why no consent banner is shown to you.
24|What we keep on your device
25|To work, the web app keeps a little information in your browser’s local storage. It does not leave your device, except what you enter offline, which is sent to the server when the network returns.

47|If you turn on notifications on this browser, it installs a small KÓMBE program that displays them and opens the app when tapped; it keeps no data. “Turn off” in your preferences removes it.
48|The Android app likewise keeps your session in the phone’s protected keychain and your offline drafts in a local database.
49|How to delete it
50|Sign out from the app, or clear the site data in your browser settings. On Android, “Clear data” for the KÓMBE app deletes everything it keeps on the phone. Warning: offline entries not yet sent would then be lost.
51|If this changes
52|If we ever had to use a non-essential cookie, it would only be set after your explicit consent, and this page would be updated.
