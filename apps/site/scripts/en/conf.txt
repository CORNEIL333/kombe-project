47|The service
48|KÓMBE collects neither your Mobile Money or bank credentials, nor your location, nor your contacts.
49|Why we process it
50|To sign you in and protect your account.
51|To keep your groups’ register and show it to members according to their roles.
52|To send sign-in codes and group notifications.
53|To ensure security, detect abuse and correct errors.
54|This processing is necessary for the service you and your group have asked for. Your data is neither sold nor used for advertising.
55|Who has access
56|The members of your group
57|, according to the transparency chosen by the group and their roles. Only the committee sees full numbers.
58|Authorised KÓMBE operators
59|, only for the requests you send to support.
60|Our technical providers
61|, only to run the service: Vercel (hosting of the application), Neon (database and sending of codes by email), Africa’s Talking (sending of SMS, when this channel is open). If you turn on notifications in your browser, its notification service (Google, Mozilla, Apple…) carries a generic message, with no amount or name.
62|Where it is hosted
63|The service is hosted by Vercel, with functions running in Europe (Frankfurt), and the database by Neon. These providers may be located outside your country; they act only on our instructions.
64|How long
65|A group’s register is kept as long as the group exists, because it underpins trust between its members.
66|Your identity can be erased on request: the group’s entries remain (so that the accounts stay accurate) but no longer designate you.
67|Requests to support are anonymised more than 730 days after they are closed.
68|Sessions expire automatically; sign-in codes are only valid for a few minutes.
69|Your rights
70|You can ask for access to your data, its correction, its export, the erasure of your identity, or object to a processing. A proportionate identity check may be requested before acting. Send your request to:
71|to be completed by the publisher
72|. You can also contact the competent data protection authority.
