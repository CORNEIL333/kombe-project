11|Our safeguards
12|Eight principles,
13|enforced by the server.
14|Sign-in
15|without a password.
16|A 6-digit code, single-use and valid for a few minutes, is sent to your address (or your number). Nothing to remember, nothing to steal over your shoulder. Sessions expire on their own.
17|Checked
18|by several people.
19|A contribution is declared by one person and confirmed by another. Nobody confirms their own declaration: the server refuses it, whatever screen is used.
20|A register
21|that is never erased.
22|Every event is added to the group’s log and chained by a SHA-256 fingerprint: changing or deleting a line breaks the chain, which shows. A mistake is corrected by a new entry.
23|Each group
24|within its own boundary.
25|A group’s data can only be read by its members, according to their roles. The separation is enforced down to the database, not just on screen.
26|Masked
27|numbers.
28|Full numbers are only visible to the group’s committee. Other members see a masked number.
29|Offline,
30|in full clarity.
31|Without a network, what you enter stays on the phone and is marked “not sent yet”. Nothing is shown as confirmed before the server answers.
32|Protected
33|in transit.
34|Exchanges go over HTTPS, with strict security headers (HSTS, content policy) that block foreign scripts.
35|Verifiable
36|minutes.
37|The minutes of a meeting are generated from the recorded events and carry a fingerprint: they are approved at the next meeting.
38|What we do not do
39|What KÓMBE
40|will never do.
41|Hold, move or block the group’s money.
42|Access your Mobile Money or bank accounts.
43|Sell your data or show advertising.
44|Set tracking cookies.
45|Give financial advice.
