15|to begin.
16|Create your account
17|No password to remember.
18|Open
19|kombe.vercel.app
20|or the Android app.
21|Enter your email address (or your number, if SMS is open).
22|Type in the 6-digit code you received: your account is created.
23|Give the name the members of your groups know you by.
24|Create a tontine
25|For the president or the founder of the group.
26|From “My tontines”, tap “Create a tontine”.
27|Follow the steps: name, frequency, contribution, order of turns, committee, rules, review.
28|Then share the invitation link (on WhatsApp, for example).
29|Join a group
30|With the code or the link received from the committee.
31|Tap “Join with a code”, or open the link you received.
32|Check the group name shown, then send your request.
33|The committee approves your membership: the group then appears in “My tontines”.
34|Declare a contribution
35|Cash, Orange Money, MTN MoMo, bank…
36|Open the group, then your dues.
37|Tap “Declare”, choose the payment method and add the reference if you have one.
38|Your declaration awaits the treasurer’s confirmation: you can see its status at any time.
39|Confirm as treasurer
40|Confirmation is done by another person.
41|Open the group’s “Contributions” tab (or the committee space on a computer).
42|Check each declaration, then confirm it or reject it with a reason.
43|You can never confirm your own declaration.
44|Working without a network
45|During a meeting with no connection.
46|Take the roll call and enter declarations as usual.
47|They stay on the phone, marked “not sent yet”.
48|They go out automatically when the network returns; nothing is shown as confirmed before that.

58|Frequently asked questions
59|Answers
60|to common questions.
61|Does KÓMBE hold the group’s money?
62|No. Money moves between members as before (cash, Mobile Money, bank transfer). KÓMBE keeps the register: who declared, who confirmed, whose turn it is.
63|I did not receive my code.
64|Check the address you entered and your spam folder, then ask for a new code. The code is only valid for a few minutes.
65|My membership is “pending”.
66|That is normal: a committee member must approve each new membership. Let them know if the wait drags on.
67|Can a contribution already confirmed be corrected?
68|Yes, through a correction added to the history. The original entry stays visible: nothing is erased silently.
69|Can I use KÓMBE on an iPhone or a computer?
70|Yes, with the web app in the browser. The committee also has the committee space, designed for a big screen.
71|Who sees my phone number?
72|Your group’s committee. Other members see your number masked.
