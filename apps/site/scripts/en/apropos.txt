11|Why KÓMBE
12|A notebook, messages,
13|doubts.
14|In many groups, the accounts live in a notebook, a spreadsheet and dozens of messages. When a doubt arises — who paid, when, how much — it weighs on the treasurer and on everyone’s trust.
15|KÓMBE replaces this fog with a single register: each contribution is declared, confirmed by another person, and stays visible. The money itself moves as before.

18|Our principles
19|What guides
20|every decision.
21|The group
22|decides.
23|The rules, the order of turns and the committee belong to the group. KÓMBE applies them; it does not invent them.
24|No money
25|with us.
26|KÓMBE is neither a bank nor a payment institution. It keeps the register and never touches the funds.
27|Nothing is erased
28|silently.
29|A mistake is corrected by a new entry; the original stays visible to all.
